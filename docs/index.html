DebtScope

Every bond a company owes, read straight from its SEC filing.

DebtScope downloads a company's annual report from the SEC, finds every bond and loan it mentions, checks that they add up to the debt the company officially reports, and turns the result into an Excel credit report and an interactive map of the filing.

What to try in the demo

Best on a computer: the demo is the full filing with a side panel.

  1. Press Start crawl

    The spider walks through the filing and spins a thread to each finding. The strip at the bottom shows what it reads in real time.

  2. Open the Debt stack tab

    Pick a note, for example the 3.600% Notes due 2042, and the spider traces every place it is mentioned.

  3. Hover a highlighted number

    Official figures show the XBRL tag behind them, such as us-gaap:LongTermDebt. Use the filter to search tags or words.

How it works

  • Data comes from SEC EDGAR: the filing itself plus the company's XBRL facts.
  • Bonds are read from the cover page, the debt footnote tables, the exhibit index and the narrative text, and every number keeps a link to the sentence it came from.
  • The bond list is reconciled with the contractual maturity schedule the company reports. When it doesn't add up, the report says so instead of filling the gap.
  • Tested on Apple and Microsoft 10-Ks, with 34 automated tests run on Windows and Linux at every change.
